The model reads the text and predicts the next word. The model does not look up a fact. The model picks a word from a list of likely words, and then the model repeats the step with the new word added to the text.
The test reads the code and checks the result. The test fails when the result is wrong. The test passes when the result is right, and the team runs the test before every merge.

The next word is chosen by chance, and the chance depends on the counts. A word that follows the word often in the text gets a high count. A word that follows the word rarely gets a low count. The temperature changes how strongly the count decides.
At a low temperature the model picks the most likely word almost every time. At a high temperature the model picks a rare word more often, and the text changes from run to run.
